# Supported platforms

[Repository home](../../README.md) · [Documentation index](../README.md)
The `0.1.0-rc.x` beta is published for **linux/amd64 only**. `x86_64` and `amd64` hosts are accepted. ARM64, macOS, Windows, rootless Docker, and orchestrators other than Docker Compose are not validated and are not advertised as supported.

Docker Engine and Compose v2 are required. Minimum evaluation capacity is 4 GB RAM, two CPU cores, and 10 GB free disk in addition to media storage. This is a beta preview, not a production-grade or high-availability distribution.
